03.

How to review

  1. Pick a pageUse the page picker at the top of any page, or the cards above.
  2. Switch breakpointDesktop shows 1440px; Mobile shows 390px.
  3. Show modulesTurn on Show design system modules to label each section with its design system module.
  4. Click aroundMenus, search, the mobile menu, cards and footer links all work.

04.

Things to know

  • FontThe design system uses Museo Sans, a licensed Adobe font. Nunito Sans stands in for it here, so letter shapes and widths differ slightly. Sizes, weights and spacing follow the design system.
  • ScopeThe prototype keeps the current template functions and content and doesn’t assume any new back-end functionality. Items that would need back-end work are marked as phase 2.
  • ContentCopy, people and navigation come from claglobal.com as it is today. Long pages are shortened.
  • ImageryPhotos and videos load live from claglobal.com. If one can’t load, a labelled placeholder takes its place.
